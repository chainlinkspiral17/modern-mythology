# VOL 7 · LAND OF MILK AND HONEY · Wiki

The synthesizing entry point for vol 7 of *Modern Mythology* —
the **Land of Milk and Honey** volume. Smolvud, Oregon, seventeen
hundred people, the last week of August into the second of
September, **2052**. The slow-sticks, the cedar bowls, the
substrate leaking out of the silicon, and the arrival of Aria.

## What vol 7 is

Twelve days on the Oregon coast. Tem Egilsdóttir has spent three
years quietly watching three people — Lena, Kai, Finn — whom the
substrate has begun to leak into: Lena's octopus compulsion,
Kai's ferret dream, the *Estuary 7* AI speaking Tem's dead
grandmother's private name. The four converge at Eddvard's cabin;
the older carriers surface one by one (Cale with thirty years of
Nate Dean's secrets, Per with the words "the Order of the Bowl,"
Roy with the missing fourth bowl, Aud, Hans, Marit, Marina from
the foundation); the four cedar bowls go warm, a hexagon of
charred wood completes, a painted face opens its eyes on an alley
wall, and at 3:02 AM on September 1st, 2052, Aria — Dickens
Nathaniel Dean's daughter — knocks on the cabin door. The
epilogue: Tem plays *Estuary 7*, is walked through the gallery of
nine builds (Dean's includes Smolvud itself), and begins her own
submission. "Vol VIII."

Tonal register: **slow / saturated / PNW**. Ninety-minute
sessions, cedar time, watching as a discipline. The volume's
argument, in Aud's word, is **the clear**: "The slow-stick does
the work the slow-stick is going to do... The clear is the work.
Live in it."

## The calendar

Present: **August 21 – September 2, 2052** (day-names in prose;
Sept 1 is a Monday). Anchors: "the last Saturday of August in
2052" (ch15_loft); "three-oh-one on the morning of the first of
September of 2052" (ch19_ring); "Tuesday, September 2, 2052"
(epilogue_morning). The substrate's "on" date: **the second week
of July 2052**, when every bowl in the network went warm at once.

## The slow-sticks

Full canon: `milk_and_honey/slowsticks.md`. In brief: dark
plastic palm-sized cartridges ("slow sticks," "SCUMM sticks"),
waxed-paper foundation sleeves, played through a **reader** and a
**cap** ("You go in through the cap, you come out through the
cap, and the cap is yours"). Ninety-minute sessions that feel
like days. The content is an **experience**; heavy players come
out changed ("The substrate gives them by taking"). Worn places
in the substrate persist as **imprints** (sixteen-year-old Nate
in Kestrel Mountain's basement; the monk; the old man at the
second pool). Objects cross out ("The substrate is not very
precise about objects that have been at a cabin recently").
Named sticks: **Kestrel Mountain** (Diego, 2034, 412 players),
the **Estuary series 1–7** (template begun by D. Dean, 1997;
*Estuary 7* by Ines Rocha, 2046), **Tideline Survey** (Ulrich
2018; Brandon T. template 2046; commercialized 2048), **Cape
Perpetua** (2023). Cale at ChillWave is the local source —
cedar shelves alphabetized by designer, sticks held for people
before he knows who they are.

## The substrate in vol 7

- **The thesis** (Tem, ch1_morning): the substrate "is supposed
  to live in the silicon... I have been wondering whether the
  substrate has started to be in the air. In the walls. In the
  wood."
- **The patches** — flat black absences that eat paint but not
  brick; the drones cannot see them; they clear the wall *ahead
  of* Lena's brush. Sites: the sanderling mural and *Starfish
  Nebula*.
- **The bowls** — Olaf's four, plus the substrate-carved fifth
  Kai carries out of Kestrel Mountain (Olaf's hand, imagery
  updated to 2052: the seven-and-a-half-armed octopus, the
  seven-floor tower). The carved figures move at cedar pace; the
  four-bowl alignment at the bakery ends with the crow saying
  "Aria" and the carved face smiling.
- **The hexagon** — nine months of charred yellow-grained wood
  from the crow; completes as six pieces in a ring, the cedar
  face in the center, one extra piece scratched **A R I A**.
- **The drones** — the foundation's overnight town maintenance
  (in off the bluff before dawn, docked by 6:30); a unit type in
  the Estuary planner; Tem's entire submission is one placed
  drone.
- **The Tower at old-Yachats** — Dean's, 2011–13, seven stories,
  sealed since 2042, entry by substrate-signature (Kai's opens
  it). Lobby portrait; the Oneironautics studio on two; the
  portal room on four ("The fourth is hers"); the garden on
  seven, where Dean "would go on waiting."
- **The resolution** — the bell rings (3:00 AM), the knock
  (3:02), each of the six sees a different form at the door, the
  wall speaks the name in front of thirty-five people, the disc
  moves itself, the traveling at the portal room, the Estuary 7
  gallery of nine builds (Dean's 2013 submission **contains
  Smolvud itself**), and Tem's submission begins: one drone,
  clock 0:01.

### The zine / SUN charcoal

The *Static Truths* beat exists **only in interlude II**: Lena
wakes with S U N in charcoal on her thigh (no date given), it
doesn't wash off for three days, she starts a four-page zine and
draws an octopus on the back cover. The main 96 scenes never
mention the zine; Lena's in-volume compulsion is the octopus
count. Treat the zine as interlude-canon, not spine-canon.

## Internal inconsistencies (the prose's own, for the author)

The scene prose contradicts itself in places; sessions must not
"fix" these silently — they are the author's to resolve:

1. **The generational strand vs. 2052.** Olaf drowns Nov '78
   with Per 18; Per is 60 in 2052 (fits ~2030 arithmetic). Marit
   has carried her 1974 bowl "fifty-one years" (→2025). Roy
   says he came "in October of 1931," was "twenty-six in '31,"
   yet has been in town twenty-one years and Hans "has known Roy
   since 2031." The Olaf/Eddvard/Marit/Olaus strand reads as a
   ~2025-present draft that was re-anchored to 2052 in some
   scenes and not others. **This is the likely origin of the
   lore files' 2025 drift.**
2. Kestrel Mountain: designed 2034 (ch11_stick) vs. 2021 poster
   (ch22_floors) vs. Nate playing it in 1993 (ch11_kestrel).
3. Brandon's death: Oct 8, 2050 vs. November 2050 vs. "three
   nights after" the third ChillWave visit.
4. Aud: retired 2030 ✓ in two places, "played it once in '52...
   the year I retired," "at this diner thirty" (→2022) vs.
   diner built 2031.
5. Kai↔Finn: "four years" vs. "fifteen years" of friendship.
6. Jameson 16 vs. 18; sanderling mural 2048 vs. '46; Lena's
   apartment above the Salty Tome vs. "on Cedar Place"; Tem 26
   vs. "nineteen years" at the skillet and "thirty-one years"
   in the cedars; Hans since 1991 vs. "thirty-one years" vs.
   "fifty-three years"; Marina since 2046 vs. "since '38";
   gallery surnames (E. Halvorsen for Eddvard Lindgren; T.
   Egilsdóttir vs. Egilsson; O. Lindholm "no relation" for
   Olaus vs. Inga Skarsgaard); Mateo's father's board "2034 to
   2018."

## Authoring rules

1. **The scene JSONs are the authority.** Wiki, artifact files,
   and Lore Hub are downstream. Cite scene files for new claims.
2. **The present is 2052.** Aug 21 – Sep 2. Do not re-derive
   2025 from the generational strand — that contradiction is
   catalogued above and reserved for the author.
3. **Do not invent named characters** into vol 7 (the Mrs. Gable
   lesson). Unnamed townspeople in the prose stay unnamed.
4. **Flag, don't fix.** New inconsistencies found in prose go in
   the list above.
