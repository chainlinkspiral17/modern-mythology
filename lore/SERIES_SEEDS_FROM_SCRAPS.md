**Reading.** Book five (2011) calls the first book "a four novellathing". The 2010 book became Vols 1–4, so *mm ii*'s "four other novellas" are most likely Vols 1–4. That suggests a pairing, offered as a lens rather than canon:
- Arcadeum complicates Vol 1 (Oneironautics, Faust).
- por puesto complicates Vol 2: the Small Wood narrator's "sophomore year spent overseas in Caracas" (`vol2_small_wood.md`).
- plannED commUNITY complicates Vol 4 (the E/D back matter and the PC stories).
- The Value Menu complicates Vol 3: it is a prequel set in the same diners-and-counters register.

### The Ariaster problem. Options for Andy

Current canon says two things. Aria is Dean's daughter, born in the future and only a flicker until SCUMM. The Arcadeum designer is June Okimoto. *mm ii* gives the designer's life to "Ariaster 'Aria' Dean". The name "Ariaster" also turns up in 2011 (a SCUMM hall-of-lore cast list) and 2012 ("Ember Ariaster takes her place at the table").

| # | Option | What it keeps | What it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** | **June takes the biography; "Ariaster" stays Aria's full given name.** June becomes a summer-of-love child (b. 1967), raised by a mechanic father across small Texas towns, toys made of radio and computer parts. She survives the 1983 bust in "a series of middle tier studios". Koa turns her down in 1991, and Oneironautics hires her in 2003. Her "latest game, hopeful big break and unmitigated disaster" is the Arcadeum present. | All of Andy's biographical text; Aria canon untouched. | June's age moves up, so she is 24 at the 1991 interview. The "upstate California in the early 80s" line becomes "Texas, late 80s", or she starts as a teenage contractor. |
| **B** | **June takes the biography, and "Ariaster" is her studio handle and the heroine of her disaster game.** The name Dean later gives his daughter is one he first saw on a June Okimoto title screen. | It explains why "Ariaster" is in the files years before Aria could exist, and plants a loop for later volumes: did the game name the girl, or did the girl name the game? | It adds a mystery that must stay unresolved. It edges toward explaining the cosmology, so keep it a single credit line. |
| **C** | **Ariaster Dean is a different Dean:** John Dean's younger sister, Dickens's aunt (b. 1967). She was raised by a mechanic, not the academic John. | It gives the Dean family a game designer before Dickens and an "ancestral link" to the craft. | It contradicts nothing directly, but it crowds the Dean tree and makes "Aria" a family name rather than Aria's own. |
| **D** | **The biography belongs to an Aria in another world.** Aria has merged across countless worlds, so in one of them she grew up with a mechanic father. | It keeps Andy's text attached to "Aria". | It breaks the flicker rule unless it is held for the SCUMM volume. It also blurs the "not every blue-haired woman is Aria" rule. |

**Recommendation:** A + B. June keeps the life; "Ariaster" survives as a credit line. *Andy decides.*

**A. *Major Arcana. 0. the fool* (2015).** https://docs.google.com/document/d/1wHNJrht23QR-q6EfZojiO8--B_VHcUK6Ls05SNDb4TY/edit?usp=drivesdk
- The narrator is the man in a 1940s French writer's story who was put on trial for believing "in a race of sentient crabs from the future." "This man was me. And or I would become him… I have cheated, I have read my own ending."

- They wreck on "the husk of an oil rig, half-consumed by monster petroleum-algae and kelp, feeding upon Louisiana coastline." Below them "giant crustaceans battled."

### 3. Refinements (tightened premises)
- **The deck's frame, in Andy's words:** "a whole generation of mostly nobodies… decay and rebirth… odd portents." Open the volume with the 2014 preface and close it, after the World, with "The water has changed and so have we."
- **The Stitchers** are the far-future cousins of Frasier's bound demons. They are teaching voices with no bodies, mending a split reality. Name them once, late, and never explain them.
- **The cupcake rival** belongs to the Empress (Nicola) chapter. A rival bakery opens across the river road and sells "one of hundreds". Nicola eats one in the car and doesn't tell anyone.

### 3. Refinements (tightened premises)
1. **Structure: the route.** The book rides **RUTA 77.** Each section is a **Stopover** (Andy's own heading: "Stopover 1"), and each stop is a character:
   - Chacaíto: Stuttgart and Timtam's market
   - Sabana Grande: TropiArepa
   - Plaza Venezuela: Chupa and Miriam
   - Petare: Torso under the unfinished highway
   - Poblano: Maribel
   - "one stop past": the painted-over last stop
2. **Register:** noir, supernatural, magic realist. The supernatural is the one-in-the-morning kind: "astral projections made flesh," a dinosaur-bird's ultraviolet canopy, a pageant queen made alien.
3. **The cover-up is the plot.** Stuttgart's pharmacy deliveries lead into the "nesting doll series of crimes." Marcus's paid-off hit-and-run is the same kind of crime at the top of the class ladder.
4. **The ticket is the MacGuffin.** Torso's lollipop-wrapper ticket passes from hand to hand down the route. Whoever holds it at the last stop rides "one stop past."
