## Voice pass (2026-10-16)

Per `/home/claude/mm_src/VOICE_PASS_BRIEF.md`. Every `[[new]]` line above was rewritten in the narrator's own voice: past tense, reported speech without quote marks, long comma-run sentences, strange things said flatly. The model was Andy's ch. xi Dean passage. Totals for the four scrap scenes: **27 lines rewritten, 1 cut.**

**Cut outright**
- field_notes: "On the due-date slip… Tem — you were right about the ending." It was marginalia pointing at another book.

**Gimmicks removed (the line kept, rewritten as an ordinary detail)**
- the_room: Dean's "It won't have me" and "only ever makes somebody else's" are now a plain account of the empty white room. It is the narrator's own memory: the hill of lights is named as the Caracas barrios, and "That's not mine / I guess it isn't" is gone.
