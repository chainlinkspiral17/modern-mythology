the hanged man

Wither and waste away, two week old fruit in a bowl speckled inside and out with this avocado-colored mold. Even t-shirts lay about in corner piles textured of crepe paper the Christmas noon after, holed discolored.

His room a place of discardment, here and there scattered about hard and soft plastic wrapper containers, reminders of disposable products all ended in respective consumer lifecycles, the admen and market peoples forced to thunk up and shuffle off a new brand of new.
