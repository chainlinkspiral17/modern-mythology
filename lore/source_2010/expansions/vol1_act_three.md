# Faust 3 — Act Three (DRAFT for Andy's review)

*New writing, not in the 2010 book. Picks up after p.86 ("Cue Text: Act Three, The Handsome Triumvirate."). Note on the text: Act Two does not stop on the broken sentence. Pages 84–86 carry on after it (Dickens fades, "the body of young Aria," the three step out into the overbright street), so the sentence is finished here as a late transmission over the lifelines rather than as a straight continuation.*

*Shirt assignments (my invention; p.81 only says "based on your artwork for each of the heroes"): Jacob = Air (he flies, p.39), Wagner = Fire, Faust = Water (his astrological element, p.29), and the fourth shirt, for Dickens, is a frame with nothing inside it (Wood, p.30).*

#### INT./EXT. THE DEEP END - NO TIME

The MUSIC aisle doesn't end. It slopes and curves downward and turns into concrete, smooth and pale, an empty swimming pool so big the far wall is out of sight, painted at the bottom with a faded blue that used to be water. The coping is lined with kids sitting in a row with their boards across their knees, waiting to drop in. All of them are dead, and none of them seem bothered by it.

Somewhere, from everywhere, there's a guitar riff, one note held, a lonesome wail.

**WAGNER**
(reverent)

He sends it. He puts the phone face down, picks up a brush and looks at it, picks a softer one, then puts that down and picks up the coarse one again.

He starts a new canvas. He doesn't know what it is yet.
