## Pizza Pirate · Smolvud, OR · vol 7

## what the prose establishes

There is a **SCUMM machine at Pizza Pirate** — a public,
cabinet-form stick reader (the at-home form is a reader and cap;
see `slowsticks.md`). A person named **Sal** — no surname given
— operates the floor and pulled the machine off it on the
volume's Thursday because *"it was acting up."*

**Wren**, fifteen, plays it: she was *"about forty minutes into
a stick I had been working on for a couple of weeks"* — public
sessions on a long-running stick, worked across visits.

The machine delivered the volume's inciting message, twice —
Tuesday, then Wednesday, when Wren wrote it in her spiral
notebook:

> *"Wren, the substrate is leaking. Tell Tem."*

Wren did not know who Tem was. Lena relayed it to Kai. Lena told
Wren to stay away from the machine. Neither Pizza Pirate nor the
machine appears again in the volume, and it is absent from the
epilogue's rendered town map.

## reserved

Who built the machine, when it arrived, who Sal is, why it
addressed Wren, and what "acting up" was — all unstated in
prose, all the author's to decide. Do not re-derive the old
draft's answers.
