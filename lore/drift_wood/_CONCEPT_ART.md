# ROFLCOPTR · concept art notes

## Sheet 1 · character + environment studies (September 2026)

*"Sample image from a later comic. Concept, not final, work in
progress."* — the user. Delivered in chat; not yet in the repo.
Suggested home when it is: `lore/drift_wood/refs/sheet_01_wood.png`
(the `lore/refs/` convention is images beside a `CREDITS.md`).

### What is on it

Watercolor-and-ink on white, laid out as a model sheet.

- **Title block.** *ROLFCOPTR* in soft slate-blue block letters,
  with a **small rainbow songbird** perched on the R at the left and
  a **helicopter rotor** on the R at the right. Left caption:
  *CHARACTER: WOOD*. Right caption: *SMALL WOOD, OR — ENVIRONMENTAL
  STUDIES*.
- **Wood.** Three studies plus two heads. Mid-forties, receding
  hairline, unkempt brown hair, stubble, hollow tired eyes with a
  faint permanent worry. Blue plaid flannel open over a dark band
  tee (an eagle/wing graphic, lettering illegible), ripped jeans,
  worn brown boots. In one he holds a tablet and reads it. Two heads
  are labeled *Fluid Expression* — the face is meant to move a lot.
- **The bird.** Small, plump, rainbow-plumaged (teal head, rose
  breast, green wing, long teal tail) — something like a painted
  bunting. Drawn at his feet with arrows to him; on the seawall; on
  the logo. It is his companion on this sheet. **There is no dog.**
- **Bait shop.** A cedar-shingle shack with a steep roof, stovepipe,
  a *BAIT SHOP* sign, a shuttered service window with a lure hanging
  in it, a barrel by the door, a small porch. Gully's, or the shack
  Todd inherited before the espresso machine.
- **Waterfront.** A trawler's masts and rigging at a wooden pier on
  pilings; fog. A **fish weathervane** on a tall pole. Street signs
  on a pole; below them a yellow square sign with a single **eye**
  drawn on it. A foggy street of gray-shingle houses running down to
  the water.
- **A three-panel strip** (Era IV):
  1. *JUST ANOTHER MORNING...* — Wood in the rain on the dock, a
     lobster trap and coiled rope beside him, reading something
     like *GLOBAL ESTATE TIDES* on the tablet.
  2. *...AND ANOTHER CONTEMPORARY PROBLEM.* — Wood at a seawall,
     head down; the bird on the wall looking at him; the bay behind.
  3. *BUT SMALL WOOD HAS ITS MOMENTS.* — Wood on a bench with a
     steaming mug, beside a **moss-covered stone head**, a giant
     carved face half sunk in the grass by a shingle wall.

### Where it agrees with the brief

Era IV Arthur: mid-forties, quiet, observant, a routine and a mug.
Small Wood as a working waterfront gone soft — tablets, "global
estate" headlines, Airbnb-era. The flannel, the boots, the fog, the
shingle, the bait shop, the dock. The three-beat rhythm (set-up,
problem, *but*). The palette (slate, sea-foam, ochre, gray).

### Art direction takeaways

- The sheet's Wood is the **Era IV** reference. Era I–III Arthur
  still needs sheets: seventeen in the wool overcoat with the
  bristol pad; twenty-six bloodshot at the CRT; thirty-four in the
  thrift-store suit on the dock.
- The **environment vocabulary** is set: cedar shingle, pilings,
  rigging in fog, street-sign poles with odd signage, moss on
  stone. Feed this to the Sunday-landscape list in `the_strip.md`.
- The **logo** is usable as the volume's title card as is, once
  the spelling is settled (see the table above).
