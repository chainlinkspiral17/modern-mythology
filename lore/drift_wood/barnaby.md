# BARNABY · and BARNABY II · the sentry
## Under the drafting table · 1999–2014 · 2014–

Barnaby does not talk. He does not have thought bubbles. His
presence is entirely behavioral — brushwork and posture, after
Watterson's observation of animal weight and motion. He is the
physical rhythm of Arthur's days and the only company in front of
whom the armor drops.

---

## I. BARNABY (1999–2014)

Scruffy, low-slung terrier-and-hound (or -beagle) mix. Folded
velvet ears. An enduring smell of dead bull kelp. Found tied to a
rusted dumpster behind the bowling alley on a freezing November
night in 1999.

**What he does.** Curls into an ammonite-tight ball under the
drafting table. Rests his chin across Arthur's slipper when the
3:00 AM self-loathing sets in. Barks maniacally at rogue waves
along the seawall. Forces Arthur out of the garage into the
sideways rain; pulls him from the unfinishable novel to watch
ospreys hunt the estuary. Lifts his head at the 509 error, sighs
once, goes back to sleep.

**The walks.** Mist-blanketed Oregon beaches. Arthur talks about
the terrors of aging, dying unknown, the light on the Sitka grove
at dawn. Barnaby shakes off salt water, snorts at kelp, trots into
the next frame, dragging Arthur with him.

**The decline (Arc 4, Nov–Dec 2014).** At sixteen he can no longer
make the steep gravel path to the ocean. Arthur lowers the drafting
table to floor height so the dog can rest his chin on his foot;
carries him down the wooden steps; the winter visit to the county
clinic. Then the Silent Sunday Trilogy:

1. **"The Carry."** Six wordless panels. Barnaby in a faded wool
   blanket along the mudflats at low tide, graying muzzle on
   Arthur's collarbone, bruised indigo sky. Arthur on a bleached
   drift cedar holding him against the wind while gulls wheel.
2. **"The Empty Chair."** (Nov 30, 2014.) The bed under the table,
   empty. Full spec in `the_strip.md`.
3. **"The Box."** (Dec 21, 2014.) Three weeks later. Full spec in
   `the_strip.md`.

Widely considered the emotional zenith of the run.

## II. BARNABY II (2014–)

Wiry, terrified, three-legged terrier/border pup with mismatched
ears — one pinned back, one up like a radar dish — coat like
unraveled steel wool, dirt on his nose. Left hind leg ends clean
below the hock, healed, tough, unbothered. Stands like a tripod,
chest out. Does not limp; scuttles with erratic, joyful momentum.

Arrives in a waxed produce box stamped ALICE'S WHOLESALE BAIT &
SMOKED MEATS on the first clear morning after three weeks of rain.
Todd's note in grease pencil on butcher paper: *Found him by the
log dump in Toledo. Vet says he's four months. His name is not my
problem.* (The bio says a timber camp near Coos Bay, 2016. Keep
Toledo and December 2014; the Sunday page is canon and Coos Bay is
what Todd tells people.)

First act: bypasses the empty dog bed completely, goes headfirst
into the scrap-paper basket. Second act: falls asleep across
Arthur's ankles with the crow-quill holder in both paws. Inherits
the post as the drawing board's sentry through April 2027, where he
charges a flock of sandpipers in the final page.

## III. THE MARK

Barnaby is the strip's signature mark made flesh. Week of "The
Empty Chair": a flat stroke. Week of "The Box": a tiny three-legged
scruff, asleep. If the volume has a stat, it is this.

## IV. THE BIRD (open)

The September 2026 concept sheet gives Arthur a small rainbow
songbird — on the seawall, on the logo's rotor, at his feet — and no
dog. Whether the bird replaces Barnaby, joins him, belongs to an
era, or is a Sunday-landscape motif (a bird that shouldn't be on
this coast, noticed) is the largest open cast question. See
`_CONCEPT_ART.md`. Barnaby's arc is too specified in the brief to
drop lightly; the bird is too charming to drop at all.
