# ARTHUR "WOODY" FINCH · the chronicler
## Small Wood, OR · born ~1980 · the strip's author

Seventeen in 1997: gaunt, slouching under an oversized thrift-store
wool overcoat, a bristol pad clutched like a shield. Editor of *The
Timberline*. Effortless draftsman. Voted Most Likely to Win a
Pulitzer. To the faculty a prodigy; to himself a brilliant exile
temporarily marooned among log trucks and estuary mist.

Fifty-ish in 2027 (the editor's round number; see the wiki's
"disagrees with itself" table): thickened hands, a routine, a
three-legged dog, ten thousand strips in boxes, at peace.

His fatal flaw, in the brief's words: **an addiction to potential.**
As long as the magnum opus remains uncompleted, it remains perfect.

## II. WHERE HE LIVES, WHERE HE WORKS

- **Childhood bedroom** (1997–?). Reserved.
- **The garage apartment** behind the cedar-shake tavern. Drafty.
  Corrugated tin roof. Douglas-fir floorboards. Casement window on
  the gravel alley. Cinderblock bookcase. Banker's boxes. Salt air
  rusting the X-Acto blades. Porch with cedar steps, aluminum storm
  door, a rail where the coffee mug goes.
- **Heceta Frame & Matting**, Highway 101. The backroom, foam core,
  a dull utility knife, the rain alley out back with the spackle
  buckets.
- **The Driftwood Motel.** Graveyard shifts, then morning shifts by
  2027. The night-audit desk. (The ice machine's hum: reserved, see
  the wiki's Echoes.)
- **Gully's Tackle & Roast.** Afternoons drawing at a table by the
  window, by 2027.

## III. WHAT HE PERFORMS, AND FOR WHOM

- With **Chloe**, he performs brilliance.
- With **Todd**, he performs irony.
- With **the internet**, he performs cynical indifference.
- With **Barnaby**, the armor drops entirely.

## IV. THE VOICE

Eight-sentence disquisitions. *"Daumier didn't need a four-year
cohort in an air-conditioned loft. Melville didn't get an MFA."*
*"If they want garbage, I will build them a cathedral of garbage."*
*"Someone has to make sure the fog doesn't get stolen."* *"I am in
the middle of a deadline, and I don't like you."* And, at the end:
*"Ten thousand days of noticing things. Nothing gets wasted unless
you weren't looking."*

The voice ages. Era I is barbed and French-cinema; Era II is
bloodshot and typed; Era III is wounded and precise; Era IV is
quiet. The VN should let the player hear the difference in the
dailies before the Sundays show it.

## V. THE PHANTOM GRAPHIC NOVEL

Rewritten since 1998. Changes genre every four years according to
whatever he is insecure about:

- 2002 — a gritty crime noir (Box 4B, 2001–03).
- 2006 — "the serious timber-faller allegory" he swears he'll return
  to after one week of ROLFCOPTR.
- 2008 — an autobiographical indie travelogue.
- 2016 — a sprawling magical-realist timber allegory.

He reworks the first thirty pages of five different novels for
years. In 2027 the editor asks where it is. He points at the box
holding the rug down.

## VI. THE CONCEPT-SHEET ARTHUR

The September 2026 sheet draws him older and balder than Era I
would suggest — receding hairline, blue plaid flannel over a band
tee, ripped jeans, worn boots, a tablet in hand, a small rainbow
bird for company. Labeled "Wood." This is Era IV Arthur. See
`_CONCEPT_ART.md`.
