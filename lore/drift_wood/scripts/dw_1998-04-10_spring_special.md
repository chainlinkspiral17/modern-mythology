## Panel 1

*full page.* A full-page drawing for the paper's spring issue. The Alsea estuary at low tide from the bluff: mudflats in long hatched bands, a channel of water catching a white sky, pilings of the old cannery dock, a heron, log booms, the far shore's spruce wall. Over-inked, ambitious, everything crosshatched. At the bottom, a hand-lettered caption explaining what the reader is looking at.

Props: mudflats; channel; cannery pilings; a heron; log booms; spruce
