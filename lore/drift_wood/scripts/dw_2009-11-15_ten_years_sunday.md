<details><summary>whole-strip prompt (concept run)</summary>

**prompt.** a full-page Sunday comic in horizontal tiers stacked top to bottom, each tier one wide panel (3 tiers). Style: comic strip panel, black brush-pen ink line with muddy early-2000s digital flat colors, rubbery kinetic linework, mid-2000s webcomic look, limited palette, slightly misregistered fills. Title of the strip: DRIFT WOOD. The strip: The arc's Sunday: the walk drawn ten times, tiny, in a grid across the top tier, one for each year; the middle tier the alley now; the last the dog under the drafting table at ten, the same as at one. Tier 1: a row of ten tiny watercolor drawings of the same man and dog walking the same alley, the style changing subtly left to right, no words. No dialogue in this tier. Tier 2: a man and a dog from behind in a rainy alley under a lit bowling alley sign, watercolor. No dialogue in this tier. Tier 3: a dog asleep under a drafting table in a garage with a lamp on and a man's hand hanging down to it, watercolor, no words. No dialogue in this tier. Lettering: every balloon and caption contains its complete text exactly as written, in clean hand-lettered comic capitals, large enough to read; size each balloon to fit its whole sentence and never cut a word at a balloon or panel edge; no words other than the ones given. Bottom right margin: tiny signature "A. Finch — Small Wood, OR" beside the gull with a small dog silhouette beside it (1999 once; 'Lanes' 2009 once).

**negative.** photorealistic, 3D render, painterly, glossy, watermark, extra fingers

</details>
