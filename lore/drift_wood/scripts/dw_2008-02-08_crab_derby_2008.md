**prompt.** a four-panel newspaper comic strip, four equal panels in a single horizontal row with thin black gutters (4 panels). Style: comic strip panel, black brush-pen ink line with muddy early-2000s digital flat colors, rubbery kinetic linework, mid-2000s webcomic look, limited palette, slightly misregistered fills. Cast, the same faces, hair and builds in every panel, ages exactly as given: WOOD — aged 28, thin man, hunched, unshaven, tired bloodshot eyes, ink on his right knuckle; GULLY — aged 29, stocky man with a round face, thick forearms, sleeves pushed up; BARNABY (the dog) — a low-slung terrier and hound mix, long body, short legs, folded velvet ears, wiry white-and-tan coat, gray on the muzzle from 2004, cloudy eyes from 2009, carried from 2010. WOOD usually wears black hoodie open over a faded band tee; the brown chore coat outdoors; dark jeans. GULLY usually wears backwards cap, red plaid flannel; the uncle's black rubber apron over it only behind the counter. Clothes, hair-dos and anything held come from this strip's description, never from the reference sheet; hands are empty unless a panel names what they hold. Title of the strip: DRIFT WOOD. The strip: Twenty-fourth annual. The flyer runs in the Newport weekly as an ad. Gully paid for the ad. Twenty bucks, plus the ad. Panel 1: a man in an apron holding a local weekly open to a quarter-page ad showing a hand-drawn crab flyer, a thin man on a stool, a dog, muddy flats (in this panel: GULLY, WOOD, BARNABY (the dog)). Gully says "Quarter page. Forty bucks. Your crab's in the paper before your strip is.". Wood says "...By two weeks.". Panel 2: close-up of a newsprint ad with a hatched crab drawing, the ink fattened by dot gain. No dialogue in this panel. Panel 3: close-up of a hand drawing a crab flyer where a small third crab sits closer to the large first one than before (in this panel: WOOD). No dialogue in this panel. Panel 4: fourteen crab flyers in a row on a shingle wall with a newspaper ad pinned beside them, a man in an apron stepping back, no words (in this panel: GULLY). No dialogue in this panel. Lettering: every balloon and caption contains its complete text exactly as written, in clean hand-lettered comic capitals, large enough to read; size each balloon to fit its whole sentence and never cut a word at a balloon or panel edge; no words other than the ones given.
