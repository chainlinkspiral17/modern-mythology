<details><summary>whole-strip prompt (concept run)</summary>

**prompt.** a full-page Sunday comic in horizontal tiers stacked top to bottom, each tier one wide panel (3 tiers). Style: comic strip panel, black brush-pen ink line with muddy early-2000s digital flat colors, rubbery kinetic linework, mid-2000s webcomic look, limited palette, slightly misregistered fills. Title of the strip: DRIFT WOOD. The strip: Twelve flyers, and the Sunday spends its tiers on the pins: the tin, the hand, the twelfth pin going in. Gully's hand, not Wood's. Tier 1: close on a small open tin of pushpins on a wooden counter, watercolor, no words. No dialogue in this tier. Tier 2: a stocky man's hand pressing a pushpin into a fresh paper flyer on a shingle wall, watercolor. No dialogue in this tier. Tier 3: a shingle wall with a row of twelve paper flyers and no one there, watercolor, no words legible. No dialogue in this tier. Lettering: every balloon and caption contains its complete text exactly as written, in clean hand-lettered comic capitals, large enough to read; size each balloon to fit its whole sentence and never cut a word at a balloon or panel edge; no words other than the ones given. Bottom right margin: tiny signature "A. Finch — Small Wood, OR" beside the gull with a small dog silhouette beside it (1999 once; 'Lanes' 2009 once).

**negative.** photorealistic, 3D render, painterly, glossy, watermark, extra fingers

</details>
