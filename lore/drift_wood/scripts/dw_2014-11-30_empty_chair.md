## Tier 1 · The Void Under the Desk

*extreme wide, low.* Tier 1 · The Void Under the Desk. Ground-level wide across the splintered Douglas-fir floorboards of the garage at 3:45 AM — exactly where the dog would have viewed the room. A battered green-enameled swing-arm lamp clamped to the tilted drafting table on the right casts a hard amber cone onto the tabletop and floor; beyond it, cool velvety indigo: banker's boxes, jars of murky brush water, an oil-stained wooden wall. Lower center, half in the light: the braided wool dog bed, worn thin to a crescent, stray white-and-tan hairs in the yarn. Beside it, half under a cinderblock bookcase, a grayed tooth-pitted tennis ball with dried estuary sand. WOOD cropped from mid-torso down on the high stool, feet on the lower rung, left foot hovering inches above where the dog's ribs would have pressed his wool sock. Perfectly still.

## Tier 2 · The Downpour on the Glass

*eye-level pan.* Tier 2 · The Downpour on the Glass. Across the drafting table out the small wood-mullioned casement window facing the gravel alley behind the tavern. Coastal storm at peak: sheets of rain dragged horizontally across the glass; beyond it an illegible bleeding blur of black Sitka spruce crowns whipping in the south wind and sodden green cedar shingles. On the sill: three worn jars — red sable brushes; rusted nibs; a single dried sand dollar. In the dark pane, the faint ghost of Wood's face: dark hollows, three days' stubble, mouth a flat exhausted line, eyes through the glass, not at it.

## Tier 3 · The Suspended Nib

*tight close.* Tier 3 · The Suspended Nib. Wood's right hand poised above the board. Zero crosshatching. Taped to the green vinyl mat with peeling kraft tape: fresh bristol, light blue-pencil four-panel grid, every panel brilliantly blank. The knuckle calloused and stained black. He grips the wooden brush handle, teeth marks near the ferrule. The sable tip fully loaded, a quarter-inch above Panel 1; a single heavy droplet of ink bowed by gravity, refusing to drop. The ink beginning to tack up on the hairs.

## Tier 4 · The Cut

*extreme wide, low.* Tier 4 · The Cut. Tier 1's framing exactly, lighting inverted. The lamp switched off. Cold desaturated pre-dawn: neutral tint, raw umber, bruised Payne's gray; the only light a faint bluish glow through the wet window. Wood a heavy slumped silhouette on the stool, head bowed, forearms flat on the angled board, shoulders hitched up around his ears. Below, in gray gloom, the empty indented dog bed.
