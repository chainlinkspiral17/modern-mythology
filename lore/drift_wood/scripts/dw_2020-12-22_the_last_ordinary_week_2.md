<details><summary>whole-strip prompt (concept run)</summary>

**prompt.** a four-panel newspaper comic strip, four equal panels in a single horizontal row with thin black gutters (4 panels). Style: comic panel, razor-sharp confident brush-and-ink line, pristine architectural perspective, crisp silhouettes, clean expressive character geometry, generous negative space. Cast, the same faces, hair and builds in every panel, ages exactly as given: WOOD — aged 40, lean man, hairline beginning to recede, weathered jaw, thickened drawing hands; GULLY — aged 41, stocky man with a round face, thick forearms, the beginnings of a beard; THE PUP (Barnaby II) — a small wiry terrier and border collie mix with THREE legs (the left hind ends clean below the hock, healed), one ear pinned back and one cocked up, coat like unraveled steel wool, gray whiskers, black nose, scuttles, stands like a tripod. WOOD usually wears blue plaid flannel open over a dark band tee, faded jeans, worn brown boots. GULLY usually wears backwards cap, faded brown canvas work jacket with the hood down over a gray hoodie; the black rubber apron over it only behind the counter. Clothes, hair-dos and anything held come from this strip's description, never from the reference sheet; hands are empty unless a panel names what they hold. Title of the strip: DRIFT WOOD. The strip: Tuesday. Coffee at Gully's. The end stool. The pup. Gully says it might rain. It rains. That's the strip. Panel 1: razor-sharp brush and ink, a thin man on a bait-shop end stool with a coffee, a stocky man in an apron, a small three-legged dog beneath, an ordinary morning (in this panel: WOOD, GULLY, THE PUP (Barnaby II)). Gully says "Might rain.". Panel 2: razor-sharp brush and ink, a shop window with rain beginning to streak it, no words. No dialogue in this panel.
