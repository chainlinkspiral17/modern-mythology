Cast, the same faces, hair and builds in every panel, ages exactly as given: WOOD — aged 26, thin man, hunched, unshaven, tired bloodshot eyes, ink on his right knuckle; GULLY — aged 27, stocky man with a round face, thick forearms, sleeves pushed up; MARIA — practical woman with a dark braid and a level face, thirties then forties, checks the dog's leg with two fingers, says 'Arthur' once, Feb 2013; BARNABY (the dog) — a low-slung terrier and hound mix, long body, short legs, folded velvet ears, wiry white-and-tan coat, gray on the muzzle from 2004, cloudy eyes from 2009, carried from 2010. WOOD usually wears black hoodie open over a faded band tee; the brown chore coat outdoors; dark jeans. GULLY usually wears backwards cap, red plaid flannel; the uncle's black rubber apron over it only behind the counter. MARIA usually wears a county-issue rain shell, jeans, rubber boots; the braid over one shoulder. Clothes, hair-dos and anything held come from this strip's description, never from the reference sheet; hands are empty unless a panel names what they hold. Title of the strip: DRIFT WOOD.
