# CHLOE STERLING · the harshest mirror
## Small Wood → Seattle / Chicago / Europe / Portland → Todd's dock

*An inference document. In the panels she is the strip's Chloe; whether the real woman is Chloe Sterling is something the bookends establish, if anything does. Lean: she writes the afterword. See `_AUTHORIAL_RULE.md`, `foreword_afterword.md`.*

Arthur's intellectual equal and the only person in Small Wood who
could deconstruct his pretenses before he finished speaking. In the
late 1990s: *The Timberline*'s culture editor, smudged black
eyeliner, a battered Discman, an unwavering determination to escape
the gravity of timber country. Where Arthur treats cynicism as an
end, Chloe uses hers as fuel.

## II. THE DYNAMIC

**With Arthur — the unpulled trigger.** When she leaves, he builds a
narrative that she abandoned their pact; when she visits, she is
furious he is rotting in Small Wood out of romanticized cowardice.
Their scenes have the highest intimacy in the strip — a private
dialect of thirty years of shared references. They never end up
together in the romantic-comedy sense. They are the curators of
each other's youth. By the 2020s the unresolved tension becomes an
unconditional sanctuary.

**With Todd — the pragmatists' alliance.** Her first stop in town,
before Arthur's chaotic studio, is Gully's. Across the counter they
discuss Arthur with the exhausted tenderness of co-parents of a
brilliant, temperamental child. She explains the shifting digital
landscape; he reports how many groceries are actually in the
fridge. No tension, only shared affection for the difficult man
who draws their lives.

## III. THE SUBVERSION

She succeeded by every metric the 1997 yearbook valued, and the
strip refuses to punish her for it or reward her with it. Her
returns *rattle the strip's foundations* — the brief's phrase — and
the volume should treat each of her arrivals as a structural event:
the pacing changes, the panel count changes, Arthur's line changes.

## IV. RESERVED

Her parents. Whether the Europe years are real or Arthur's
embellishment. What she does after 2021. Whether she ever moves
back, or only ever visits.
