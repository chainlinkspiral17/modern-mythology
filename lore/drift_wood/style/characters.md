# CHARACTERS · model sheets by era

Ages follow the strip's own math (Wood born 1980). Hero-slot ids
match `godot/tools/comic/heroes_vol10.json`. Each block ends with a
**sheet prompt** — a model-sheet generation note; prefix it with the

## WOOD · the strip's lead

**Never changes:** thin. Slouch that becomes a stoop. Sharp jaw.
Dark hair that is never combed. Hands drawn with more care than the
face. The margin signature is his, but *he* is never drawn signing.

**The rule:** Wood is drawn *taller than the author* in 1997 and
*smaller than himself* in the six weeks after the meme (two-thirds
size, everything else normal). From 2021 he is absent, then from
behind, then a reflection. See `eras.md` → devices.

### wood_17 · 1997–1999 · The Timberline
- Gaunt, seventeen, drawn a head taller than he should be.
- Oversized thrift-store wool overcoat, worn indoors, to the knee.
  Faded black tee. Dark jeans. Sneakers.
- Bristol pad clutched flat to the chest like a shield.
- Posture: declaiming. One arm out. Chin up.
- Expression range: fervent · deadpan · deflated · caught.
- Props: the pad; a cafeteria tray; a red pen (hers, borrowed).
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet, three-quarter front, profile and
  back views plus four expression heads, of a gaunt seventeen-year-
  old boy in an oversized wool overcoat to the knee holding a
  bristol drawing pad flat against his chest, unkempt dark hair
  falling forward, sharp jaw, sneakers; expressions: fervent,
  deadpan, deflated, caught.*

### wood_20 · 1999–2003 · the weekday daily
- Twenty to twenty-three. Same slouch; the coat has changed: a
  brown canvas **chore coat**, frayed cuff, one mismatched button,
  an ink stain on the pocket. This coat lasts until 2011.
- Three days' stubble. Hair longer.
- Props: a dull utility knife; a Datsun's steering wheel; a
  stapler; the chore coat's pockets, always occupied by hands.
- Posture: hands in pockets. Sits on spackle buckets.
- Expression range: dead-eyed · earnest · blank · wounded.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a thin young man in his early
  twenties in a worn brown canvas chore coat with a frayed cuff and
  a mismatched button, stubble, dark uncombed hair, hands in
  pockets; front, profile, seated-on-a-bucket pose, plus expression
  heads: dead-eyed, earnest, blank, wounded.*

### wood_26 · 2004–2011 · the webcomic years
- Twenty-four to thirty-one. Hunched at a CRT. Bloodshot when the
  strip is about the internet. Black hoodie open over a band tee
  with a faded eagle. The chore coat outdoors.
- Ink permanently on the right middle finger's knuckle.
- Props: the CRT; the mouse; a two-liter of generic citrus soda; a
  paper tray of corn in September.
- Posture: turned toward a screen even when there isn't one.
- The two-thirds weeks (Nov 16 – late Dec 2006): identical
  costume, drawn at 2/3 scale in normal-scale panels. Do not
  shrink the props.
- Expression range: manic · resigned · bemused · wry.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a thin man in his mid-twenties,
  hunched, black hoodie open over a dark band tee with a faded
  eagle, ink-stained right hand, tired eyes; front, profile, seated
  at a CRT pose, plus a second figure of the same man at two-thirds
  scale beside a normal-scale dog; expression heads: manic,
  resigned, bemused, wry.*

### wood_34 · 2012–2020 · the peak
- Thirty-two to forty. The line is razor-sharp now and so is he:
  the hairline beginning to recede, the jaw weathered, the hands
  thickened.
- **Blue plaid flannel** open over a dark band tee. Faded jeans.
  Worn brown boots. This is the concept sheet's costume, ten years

- Expression range: quiet · flat · trapped · zero-sentimentality ·

- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a lean man in his mid-thirties

## CHLOE · the strip's Chloe

**Never changes:** the eyeliner (heavy, then smudged, then faint,
then gone by 2024). Dark hair. A posture that is straighter than
everyone else's. She is drawn *better than the room she's in*. Her
handwriting — tiny cramped capitals — is a costume element: it
appears on postcards, notes, the yearbook line, and is the
lettering the strip uses whenever she writes.

**Rule:** her returns rattle the strip. Every arrival changes the
panel count or the line. She is never drawn from behind until
2002, and from then on the from-behind view is how the strip
leaves her.

### chloe_17 · 1997–2000
- Smudged black eyeliner. Straight dark hair to the shoulders.
  Oversized dark cardigan. A **Discman** clipped to her belt,
  headphones around her neck or one ear off. A paperback with the
  cover folded back. A red pen.
- Posture: reading while talking. Doesn't look up.
- Expression range: dry · level · furious-quiet · one-panel-tender.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a seventeen-year-old girl with
  heavy smudged black eyeliner, straight dark shoulder-length hair,
  an oversized black cardigan, a portable CD player on her belt
  with headphones around her neck, a paperback folded open in one
  hand, a red pen in the other; front, profile, seated-reading
  pose; expression heads: dry, level, quietly furious, tender for
  one panel.*

### chloe_28 · 2002–2010
- Short hair now, expensive. A **camel wool coat** (2008: the only
  warm color on the mill page). Later the coat is Portland-gray.
  City boots. The eyeliner smudged, lighter.
- 2002 (age 21, the dock): the transitional look — hair just cut,
  a real coat, the eyeliner still heavy.
- Posture: one hand in the coat pocket. Sits on docks with her
  feet over the water.
- Expression range: dry · hollow-beginning · fond · exhausted-
  tenderness (toward Gully, about Wood).
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a woman in her late twenties
  with short expensive dark hair, lighter smudged eyeliner, a camel
  wool coat, city boots, one hand in her coat pocket; front,
  profile, back (leaving), seated on a dock edge; expression heads:
  dry, fond, hollow, exhausted tenderness.*

### chloe_34 · 2013–2020
- Hair pinned up. **Tailored gray cashmere**, slim dark trousers.
  The ring (a plain band, small stone) from March 2013 — drawn as a
  dot for one week, then correct. The eyeliner faint.
- 2014, the dock: the wedding gown, heels kicked off and set aside,
  the hem on wet cedar.
- Posture: straight. Holding a cup with both hands when it's cold.
- Expression range: level · burnout-hollow · fond · "you're a
  coward, Arthur Finch."
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a woman in her mid-thirties with
  dark hair pinned up, faint eyeliner, a tailored light gray
  cashmere sweater and slim charcoal trousers, a plain ring with a
  small stone; front, profile, back, plus a second figure in a
  simple wedding gown barefoot holding her heels; expression heads:
  level, hollow, fond, cutting.*

### chloe_44 · 2021–2027
- Gray in the dark hair. No makeup. A worn **navy rain jacket**,
  jeans, olive rubber boots. A ceramic mug in both hands.
- Posture: sitting on Gully's dock. Sitting on the final page.
- Expression range: clear · unhurried · what-thirty-years-does.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a woman in her mid-forties with
  dark hair streaked gray and no makeup, a worn navy rain jacket,
  jeans and olive rubber boots, holding a white ceramic mug in
  both hands; front, profile, seated on a dock; expression heads:
  clear, unhurried, wry, at rest.*

## GULLY · the strip's Gully

**Never changes:** stocky. Backwards ball cap (until the day he
takes it off: Feb 2001, the certificate, and June 2009, the
wedding; and for good sometime after 2020). Easy face. Ten-second
delay. Muddy work boots. His block-capital handwriting in grease
pencil on butcher paper.

**Rule:** Gully is always the right size. When Wood shrinks, Gully
doesn't. When Wood is a hand at a door, Gully is a whole man on the
step.

### gully_17 · 1997–2002
- Backwards blue cap. Faded green hoodie, hands in the front
  pocket. Tan work boots, muddy. Round face.
- Props: a napkin and a pen (he draws — walruses, gulls); two
  grease sacks; a maple bar; a crab pot; a thermos.
- Posture: slouch; back row; on a dock coiling rope.
- Expression range: cheerful · plain · content · absorbed
  (drawing).
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a stocky teenage boy in a blue
  baseball cap worn backwards, a faded green hoodie with his hands
  in the front pocket, muddy tan work boots, round easy face;
  front, profile, seated-drawing-on-a-napkin pose; expression
  heads: cheerful, plain, content, absorbed.*

### gully_27 · 2002–2011 · the apron
- The uncle's **black rubber apron** over a red plaid flannel,
  sleeves pushed up, forearms thick. The cap. From 2006, sometimes
  pen on the forearm (MARIA — 4PM).
- Props: the two-group espresso machine; the giant thermos and
  paper cups; the twenty-dollar bill; a flatbed's keys; a carving
  knife (2009).
- Posture: behind the counter, both hands on it. Kneeling to a
  dog.
- Expression range: grin · careful (the father) · steady ·
  delighted.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a stocky man in his late
  twenties in a black rubber fisherman's apron over a red plaid
  flannel with the sleeves pushed up, a backwards cap, thick
  forearms; front, profile, behind-a-counter pose, kneeling-to-a-
  dog pose; expression heads: grin, careful, steady, delighted.*

### gully_35 · 2012–2020 · the canvas jacket
- **Faded brown canvas Carhartt-style work jacket**, hood down. The
  cap. A gray hoodie under. Breath pluming in cold panels.
- Props: the flatbed; a winch; a doughnut spare; a sheet cake; a
  produce box; a butcher-paper note; a child on his shoulders; a
  key to the frame shop.
- Posture: walking away with hands in pockets, not looking back.
  That walk is a set piece (Dec 2014, Dec 2016).
- Expression range: tired kindness · "Hm." · not looking back.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a stocky man in his mid-thirties
  in a faded brown canvas work jacket with the hood down, a gray
  hoodie beneath, backwards cap, jeans, tan boots; front, profile,
  and the key pose: walking away down a gravel drive with hands in
  pockets, seen from behind; expression heads: tired kindness,
  thoughtful 'hm', easy.*

### gully_48 · 2021–2027
- Heavyset. Gray in the beard. **No cap.** A green flannel and a
  black fleece vest. Rubber boots. On the dock.
- Props: the mug; the dock; the chimney's smoke on the final page.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a heavyset man in his late
  forties with gray in his beard and no hat, a green flannel and a
  black fleece vest, jeans and rubber boots, standing on a dock
  pointing at the water; front, profile, seated on a dock edge.*

## THE DOGS

### barnaby · 1999–2014
- Low-slung terrier-hound mix. Long body, short legs. **Folded
  velvet ears.** Wiry white-and-tan coat. Smells of dead bull kelp
  (drawn as a faint wavy line when wet).
- Drawn in four strokes when moving, six when old. Ages honestly:
  gray on the muzzle from 2004; cloudier eyes from 2009; cataracts
  by 2012; carried from 2010.
- Signature poses: the ammonite ball under the table; chin on the
  slipper / foot; barking at a wave; at Gully's hip.
- **Never** a thought bubble. **Never** talks.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a scruffy low-slung terrier-
  hound mix with a long body, short legs, folded velvet ears and a
  wiry white-and-tan coat; standing, curled in a tight spiral,
  chin resting on a slipper, mid-bark at a wave, being carried in a
  blanket; plus three heads at ages one, eight and fifteen with
  the muzzle graying and the eyes clouding.*

### barnaby_ii · 2014–2027
- Wiry terrier/border mix. **Three legs**: the left hind ends clean
  below the hock, healed, unbothered. One ear pinned back, one
  cocked up like a radar dish. Coat like unraveled steel wool.
  Gray whiskers, black leather nose.
- Does not limp. **Scuttles** with erratic joyful momentum. Stands
  like a tripod, chest out. Sleeps across ankles. Chews the wooden
  crow-quill holder (dented from day one).
- Signature poses: headfirst in the wastebasket; standing in the
  produce box; asleep on a foot with the nib holder.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a small wiry-haired terrier-
  and-border-collie mix with three legs, the left hind leg ending
  cleanly below the hock, one ear pinned back and one upright,
  steel-wool coat, gray whiskers; standing like a tripod with
  chest out, mid-scuttle with motion lines, asleep across a man's
  ankles holding a wooden pen holder, standing inside a cardboard
  produce box; puppy and adult heads.*

### the_bird · 2022–2027 · ROFLCOPTER only
- Small, plump, rainbow: teal head, rose breast, green wing, long
  teal tail. A painted bunting on a coast that has none.
- On the seawall; on a piling; on the sill; on the logo's R.
- Never explained. Not in the public strip. Not in the margin.
- **Sheet prompt:** *model sheet of a small plump songbird with a
  teal head, rose-pink breast, green wing and long teal tail,
  perched on a weathered seawall, on a dock piling, on a window
  sill beside three jars, and in flight; soft watercolor.*
