# LOCATIONS · hero sets

Fixed details are canon (the scripts depend on them). "Changes"
lists what the strip alters over time so a 2003 panel and a 2019
panel of the same place differ correctly. Location ids match the
`location` field in the strip JSONs and the `locales_needed` list in
`heroes_vol10.json`.

---

## dw_cafeteria · Small Wood High cafeteria
- **Fixed.** Long tables. A serving line with a sneeze guard and a
  heat lamp. Steel pans (corn, peas). A vending machine by the
  door. A wall of tall windows onto rain. Fluorescent glare.
- **Changes.** 1997: everyone faces one direction (the front).
  2017: new tables, a card-reading vending machine, everyone faces
  down (phones). The first lunch lady never looks up; the 2017 one
  does.
- **Rhyme.** #1's last panel: Wood alone at the near end of a long
  table; the strip returns to it every September.
- **Prompt:** *a small-town high school cafeteria: long laminate
  tables, a serving line with a sneeze guard and a heat lamp over
  steel pans, a vending machine by the door, a wall of tall windows
  with rain on them, flat fluorescent light.*

## dw_timberline_newsroom · the school paper's room
- **Fixed.** Two desks pushed together under one lamp. A paste-up
  board with masking tape. A light table. One beige computer. A
  dark window. A hand-lettered poster (THANKFUL FOR, faded a year
  later).
- **Set pieces.** The taped blank rectangle (#4 and #37). Red pen on
  the drawing. Boxes and an unplugged computer (#38).
- **Prompt:** *a cramped 1990s high school newsroom: two desks
  pushed together under a single lamp, a paste-up board with strips
  of masking tape, a light table, a beige computer with a CRT, a
  black night window, a faded hand-lettered poster.*

## dw_newport_lot · the community college, Newport
- **Fixed.** A rain-slick parking lot. A sign: LOT C. Spruce at the
  edge. An old Datsun (1999–2011). A campus security windbreaker.
- **Also.** The print shop (a photocopier the size of a car, warm
  stacks), the Composition II classroom (adults of all ages, a
  whiteboard: THESIS → EVIDENCE → CONCLUSION), the college
  cafeteria (2001: nobody facing the same direction).
- **Prompt:** *a rainy community college parking lot on the Oregon
  coast, a LOT C sign, an old Datsun parked alone, spruce at the
  lot's edge, low institutional buildings.*

## dw_laundromat · the laundromat on main street
- **Fixed.** Dryers along one wall. A folding table. The free rack
  by the door: real-estate flyers, a church bulletin, and (1999–
  2003) the digest between them. A door beside the rack, unmarked,
  that is (for the reader of vol 7) Pizza Pirate's back-hall door.
- **Prompt:** *a small-town laundromat: a row of dryers, a folding
  table, a wire free-paper rack by the glass door holding flyers
  and a small stapled zine, an unmarked side door, rain in the
  window.*

## dw_frame_shop · Heceta Frame & Matting, Highway 101
- **Fixed.** A low roadside building, gravel lot, a hand-lettered
  HOURS card in the window. Inside: frames on every wall, a mat
  cutter, a green vinyl cutting mat, foam core, a radio, a counter
  with sample corners. A side door onto the **rain alley**: two
  upturned five-gallon spackle buckets.
- **Changes.** HOURS: Tue/Thu/Sat (2000) → TUES · SAT · 10–3 (2005)
  → CLOSED SAT (2011). The green swing-arm lamp leaves with Wood in
  May 2011. Gully has a key from 2011 (the furnace). Framed things
  the strip made here: a fishing license (cedar), a 1971 wedding
  photo, a child's drawing (red), a death certificate (cedar), a
  wedding invitation (cedar, cream-and-green mat).
- **Prompt:** *the interior of a small roadside picture-framing
  shop: sample frames covering the walls, a mat cutter on a green
  vinyl mat, stacks of foam core, a counter with corner samples, a
  hand-lettered hours card taped in the front window; and a side
  door onto a narrow rain alley with two upturned spackle buckets.*

## dw_garage · the garage apartment behind the tavern · 2004–2027
- **Fixed.** Cedar-shingle walls. Corrugated tin roof (rain on it
  is a sound the strip draws). Douglas-fir floorboards, splintered.
  A small wood-mullioned **casement window** facing the gravel
  alley behind the tavern. The **drafting table** (uneven leg —
  shimmed with a licensing letter from 2007). The **green
  enameled swing-arm lamp** (from 2011; before that a cheap desk
  lamp). A cinderblock bookcase. Banker's boxes, numbered (1–40 by
  2026). A wicker scrap basket. Jars of murky brush water. The
  **corkboard** (see `objects.md`). #1's original pinned to the
  wall (from Sept 2004). "Lanes" framed on the wall (2009). The
  invitation framed beside it (2014). The braided wool **dog bed**
  under the table (2004–2027; empty Dec 2014 – Jan 2016; the pup's
  from Jan 2016).
- **The sill.** Two jars (brushes, nibs) from 2006; the third (a
  sand dollar from Beverly Beach) from May 2013.
- **The porch.** Three cedar steps. An aluminum storm door. A rail
  where the mug goes. A sloped gravel driveway down to the
  highway. Frost on the puddles in December.
- **Changes.** The table lowered to a foot off the floor Nov 2014 –
  Jan 2015. The produce box kept on the porch from 2015. The
  monitor: CRT (2004) → flat (2012) → a laptop (2015). The chorus
  on the back wall (2004–2011).
- **Prompt (interior):** *a garage apartment studio: cedar-shingle
  walls, a corrugated tin ceiling, splintered fir floorboards, a
  drafting table with a green enameled swing-arm lamp under a small
  wood-mullioned casement window, three jars on the sill, a
  cinderblock bookcase, numbered banker's boxes, a wicker
  wastebasket, a crowded corkboard, an old comic strip pinned to
  the wall, a braided wool dog bed under the table.*
- **Prompt (porch):** *a small porch with three weathered cedar
  steps and an aluminum storm door, a rail, a sloped gravel
  driveway descending to a coastal highway, Sitka spruce, frost on
  the puddle edges.*

## dw_tavern · the cedar-shake tavern (unnamed)
- **Fixed.** Cedar shake. A beer sign. Three pickups. The sign
  above the door with its name weathered to a wood-grain blur (never
  legible — reserved until the 2025 ROFLCOPTER sequence). Inside:
  a bar, three regulars on stools since the mill closed, a jukebox
  with one Springsteen that plays at nine. Its back wall is the
  garage's view.
- **Prompt:** *a cedar-shake roadside tavern at evening with a beer
  sign and three pickup trucks, the name on the sign above the door
  weathered into an unreadable blur, a garage roofline behind.*

## dw_boat_launch → dw_gullys · the shack, then Gully's Tackle & Roast
- **Fixed.** A shingle shack at the boat launch. A wooden dock on
  pilings. Crab pots. The **lawn chair** (green webbing, rusted
  frame) under the eave. The crab flyers on the wall, one per year
  from 1998 (see `objects.md`). The bay.
- **1998–2002 (the uncle's):** a hand-painted CRAB DERBY sign; the
  oxygen line from July 2000; the chair empty from Feb 2001.
- **Nov 2002:** the chrome two-group espresso machine arrives.
- **June 2003 on (Gully's):** the finished hand-painted sign
  GULLY'S TACKLE & ROAST; a chalkboard menu in a crab pot
  (NIGHTCRAWLERS · ESPRESSO · CHOWDER (SAT) · BAIT · DAY-OLD MAPLE
  BARS); the counter with the machine between the bait cooler and
  the register; **one stool** (Wood's, the end one); the
  **corkboard above the register** (starts with one pencil rough,
  fills for twenty-four years); a giant thermos and paper cups; the
  dog's basket on the counter (2014).
- **The dock.** Where the strip's most important conversations
  happen: 2002, 2005, 2014 (the wedding), 2024 (the Rainier). The
  Fourth of July from here every year.
- **Prompt:** *a weathered shingle shack at a small-town boat launch
  with a hand-painted sign reading GULLY'S TACKLE & ROAST, a
  wooden dock on pilings, crab pots, a chalkboard menu propped in a
  crab pot, an old green-webbed lawn chair under the eave, and
  inside a counter holding a chrome two-group espresso machine
  between a bait cooler and an old register, a single stool, a
  crowded corkboard above the register.*

## dw_motel_desk · the Driftwood Motel · 2011–2027
- **Fixed.** A low L of rooms off 101. A pole sign: DRIFTWOOD MOTEL
  · VACANCY, pale neon. The lobby: a night-audit desk, a CRT, a
  bell, a key rack, a vending machine, a glass door onto the lot.
  The **ice machine** down the hall: its hum is a flat line under
  every panel here.
- **Wood's kit.** The maroon polo (a size too big). Registration
  cards (he draws on their backs). The pad.
- **Prompt:** *a small roadside motel lobby at night: a night-audit
  desk with a CRT and a desk bell, a key rack, a vending machine, a
  glass door onto a dark parking lot, a dim hallway with an ice
  machine.*

## dw_bowling_alley · Small Wood Lanes
- **Fixed.** Across 101 from town. A neon sign on the roofline:
  SMALL WOOD LANES, pink and blue. A gravel lot. Behind: the
  **dumpster** (rusted, chained) beside a chain-link fence under a
  bare bulb — where Barnaby was tied, Nov 1999. A wet ring of rope
  on the fence (1999–2003; gone by 2004). The bulb burns out 2007,
  replaced 2008. The dumpster replaced by a blue recycling bin in
  2017; its rust footprint stays on the gravel.
- **The sign's letters.** See `objects.md` for the year-by-year.
- **Prompt:** *a small-town bowling alley seen across a two-lane
  highway at night, a pink and blue neon sign on the roofline, a
  gravel lot; and behind it a rusted chained dumpster beside a
  chain-link fence under a single bare bulb.*

## dw_beach · the beach, the spruce grove, the drift log
- **Fixed.** A long Pacific Northwest beach below a bluff; a
  **steep gravel path** down to it. Kelp. Sandpipers. A **spruce
  grove** at the beach's edge that the light comes through for
  twenty minutes. A **bleached drift cedar log** (from 2007) where
  they sit. The surf as a white line.
- **Grunion night.** Lanterns, buckets, half the town at the surf
  line, silver fish on wet sand, a banner, a Grunion Queen on a
  flatbed, later Gully's thermos line.
- **Prompt:** *a long gray Pacific Northwest beach below a bluff
  with a steep gravel path, kelp on wet sand, sandpipers, a dark
  spruce grove at the beach's edge with light breaking through it,
  a massive bleached drift cedar log, the surf a white line.*

## dw_mudflats · Alsea Bay at low tide
- **Fixed.** Mudflats in long bands, a channel catching the sky, the
  old cannery pilings, log booms, a heron, the far shore's spruce
  wall, the bay mouth opening to the Pacific. Seen from the bluff
  or walked.
- **Set pieces.** The Spring Special (1998, captioned). The two-
  thirds Sunday (2006). "The Carry" (2014). The final page (2027).
- **Prompt:** *a Pacific Northwest estuary at low tide: long bands
  of mudflat, a channel of water reflecting the sky, old cannery
  dock pilings, log booms, a heron, a far wall of spruce, the bay
  mouth opening to the ocean.*

## dw_cedar_mill · the abandoned cedar mill · closed 1994
- **Fixed.** A long sagging shed. A brick smokestack. Blackberry
  over the loading dock. A chain across the drive. Inside: light in
  long bars through missing roof panels, the great saw, sawdust.
- **Changes.** 2016: a cedar sapling in the stack's rim (real);
  the Cedar Sundays grow it into a tree (imagined; the chain is the
  tell). 2023: the ROFLCOPTER mill run draws its last year, 1994.
- **Prompt:** *an abandoned cedar mill: a long sagging shed, a brick
  smokestack, blackberry overtaking the loading dock, a rusted
  chain across the drive; inside, light falling in long bars
  through missing roof panels onto a rusted saw and drifts of
  sawdust.*

## dw_main_street · Small Wood's main street
- **Fixed.** Four blocks (three and a half in Oct 2000). The

  log truck passing, half-dead Christmas lights in December.*

## dw_pavilion_dock · the San Juan Islands wedding · July 2014
- **Fixed.** A minimalist cedar-and-glass pavilion over Puget Sound.

## dw_seattle_hill · Seattle, as imagined · Sept 2000
- **Fixed.** Drawn from a postcard and nerve: a steep hill of wet
  brick, buildings taller than any in the strip, a gallery window
  with something made of light, a different rain, one umbrella.
  The strip's most beautiful drawing of a place the author never
  saw.
- **Prompt:** *a steep city hill of wet brick with tall buildings
  and a glowing gallery window, fine rain, two figures under one
  umbrella, drawn from imagination rather than reference.*

## ROFLCOPTER-only sets (2021–2027)
- **dw_library_back_room** — the town library's back room: archive
  boxes, an empty chair (the librarian's), reading-room glass in
  which Wood is a reflection, his own strips in a folder.
- **dw_stone_head** — a moss-grown carved stone face by a shingle
  wall near the water, half sunk in grass, a bench beside it. The
  concept sheet's landmark. Drawn from every side in 2024–25.
- **dw_the_ridge** — the forested ridge east of town where the glow
  has been since 2009; in the private pages, drawn closer than the
  strip ever went; the sequence stops before it arrives.
- **the eye sign** — a yellow square sign with a single eye on a
  street pole, with a fish weathervane above (concept sheet).
- **the route north** — 101's bends past Newport, Eugene, the
  Greyhound's road to Seattle, page by page.
- **Chloe's rooms** — a Seattle apartment empty with boxes by the
  door (2021); Chicago; a hotel in Lisbon; drawn from postcards
  and nothing.
