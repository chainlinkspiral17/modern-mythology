# THE SMALL WOOD VOLUMES · canonical reference files

The volume family set in **Small Wood, Oregon** — the coastal
town of the DRIFT WOOD strips. Era: the late 1990s (Class of
1998), the master timeline's long-open vol 4-era window.

## sourcing rule

Author-supplied art and pages are the authority for this volume
family (the way the scene JSONs are for vol 7). The markdown
files are downstream catalogues: transcribe, organize, flag —
never silently resolve an ambiguity the art leaves open. Open
questions live in each section file and, where they touch other
volumes, in `lore/_MASTER_TIMELINE.md`.

## reserved

The rest of the Small Wood volumes' shape — how many sections,
whether Driftwood's "first year" implies further runs, and the
relationship of this town to COMMUNITY PLANNED's `small_wood`
region and to vol 7's coastline — is the author's, pending more
material.
