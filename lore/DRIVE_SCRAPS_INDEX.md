# Drive Scraps Index: odd stories and fragments for *Modern Mythology*

Compiled 2026-10-16 from Andy Link's Google Drive. Access was strictly read-only (search, read, metadata); nothing in Drive was created, changed, shared or trashed.

**Method.** I listed every Google Doc Andy owns (353 docs, no further pages). I also listed his owned docx/txt/rtf/odt files, his pre-2023 PDFs, and his Sheets/Slides. On top of that I ran full-text searches for series terms (Oneironautics, Dickens Dean, Small Wood, Arcadeum, Triumvirate, Graustark, Smolvud, Ariaster, Harmony Creek, por puesto, "by andy link") and checked shared-with-me files. Every candidate was opened. Each Untitled document was triaged with a content snippet and then read in full if it looked like fiction.

**Excluded.** Everything in `meta/_DIGEST.md`, `meta/`, `arcana_src/`, and the current manuscripts. Those appear only in the "Already used" table at the end.

**Conventions.**
- Word counts are estimates.
- "c" = Drive createdTime and "m" = modifiedTime. Many 2008–2010 docs were bulk-imported on 2012-06-04/10, so for those the **modified** date is the real writing date.
- "Personal — not for use" items are listed by title only.

**Count.** 105 distinct Drive files are indexed below, grouped into about 80 entries. On top of that, about 20 personal titles are listed without contents, about 25 items are noted as already used or superseded, and about 25 third-party, work or admin items were skipped.

## Top 10 picks (ranked by fit)

1. **Oneironautics is dedicated to the art…** (2010). The in-world studio prospectus. It pitches *Land of Milk & Honey* as a 3-player game where the players are the **Artist, Musician and Philosopher** defending a slacker utopia from Business, Military and Religion.
