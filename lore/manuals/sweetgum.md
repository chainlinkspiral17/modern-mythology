# sweetgum
### no publisher · 1996 · one night
### the watchman

---

## the box copy

*there is no box. if you got this cart it came from a table at a
zine fair or from somebody who trusted you with it. this page is
the manual. photocopy it before you lose it.*

*you are the night watchman at a summer camp. the campers are
asleep. the watch runs 9 pm to 6 am. that's the whole game and i
mean that.*

---

## getting started

this cart doesn't show up on a shelf until you know why it exists.
if you can see it, you already know. boot it. the watch starts at
21:00.

**controls:** the camp is the map on your screen. click a place
connected to where you stand and you walk there. walking takes as
long as walking takes. [ CHECK PADLOCK ] checks the padlock. the
log is on the right. esc puts the watch down, but the watch
doesn't really go down.

## the rounds

eight stations. gate · mess · boathouse · cabins one through four
· the waterline. a round means you stood at each one and checked
it.

rounds are due every hour. nothing enforces this. nothing will
stop you from sitting at the gate all night. the log will simply
show the gaps, and the log is forever, so.

## the log

the log is the job. TIME and STATION fill themselves. CONDITION
is yours — type what you see, press enter or [FILE].

two things you should know before your first entry:

1. **the log keeps everything.** it does not clear between nights
   or between owners of this cart. when you open it you will find
   the entries of everyone who stood this watch before you, going
   back years. mine are in there. you'll know which ones.
2. **write what the form asks for.** time. station. condition.
   the form knows what it has fields for.

## the duty

the watch is 9 pm to 6 am, about forty minutes of your actual
sitting-there time. mostly nothing happens. that is what a good
watch is. walk your rounds. check the locks. file your entries.
the crickets do what crickets do. the clock is louder than you'd
think.

if you get tired, that's correct. stay awake anyway. that's the
job. somebody has to have this job. that's why i made this.

## the morning

at 6 am the sky line brightens and the watch is over. the log
closes itself out. hand the cart to somebody you trust, or stand
the watch again — the camp doesn't mind, and the log has room.

i'm not going to tell you anything else about the night. the
manual teaches the rounds and the log. the night teaches the rest,
or it doesn't, and either way you file your entries.

## the fine print

*this game was made by one person on one machine with one color of
ink. there are mistakes in it. they stay. it is not for sale and
it was never for sale, so if you paid for it, get your money back
and tell me who charged you. the camp is not a real camp. the date
on the log header is a real date. stay awake. that's all the
instructions there are, and there were never any others.*
