## PART I.5 · THE ARCANA LAYER

The Major Arcana is the deck's oldest structural stratum, and it
reconciles cleanly across every volume:

- **1870s, Bern** — the Arcana carved on the Kestrel Mountain
  fountain rim by "a hand from Bern" — *"a kind of
  bibliography"* — the same city, era, and craft lineage as the
  bowls network's origin (1870) and Friedrich Schenck, the Bern
  carver-turned-printer. **[REC]** The arcana carving and the
  listening network share one origin layer; the deck's card
  structure is what the network has been listening *through*.
- **vol 5 (~2019)** — the Arcana as the Graustark cast: the
  Fool's Journey "maps onto John Frank's vol 5 arc most
  directly," fractured across the cast (`_TAROT_LORE.md` —
  John/Fool at the D'Ambrosio diner, Frasier/Magician, Elicia/
  Priestess, Nicola+Aria/Empress split POV, Dante/Emperor…).
  TAROT GAUNTLET is this layer's game.
- **~2018, between vol 3 and vol 5** — *The Pomegranate Hour*:
  Elicia's 22 episodes on VEIL.tv, one per Arcana, "filmed
  within a 60-mile radius of one unnamed Texas exurb" (Harmony
  Creek), each foreshadowing vol 6 and vol 7. In-episode props
  anchor the window: the NEXCORP COMMUNITY GRANT **2018**
  plaque; the 1996 landline as a deliberate prop. The domain
  lapses in vol 5 (~2019). The RES_SOL_VERIFY viewer is "the
  deck's clearest preview that vol 6 is already watching vol 5."
- **vol 6 (2025–26)** — the Arcana goes underground: the CP
  circle's registers, the demons, Dean reading MAINSTREET; the
  gauntlet arcana locales persist as Graustark geography.
- **vol 7 (2052)** — the Arcana resurfaces carved: Marit's bowl
  carries wand, star, chariot, the hanged man, the tower; the
  fountain waits at Kestrel Mountain's exit; "the cards are
  still here but by chapter sixteen the characters notice them
  themselves." John Frank's *The Frequency Beneath* — the
  Fool's own book — is what Tem reads aloud on Sundays.

Cross-check that now holds: the vol 5 window (~2019, Sinkhole
seven years before vol 6) fits the Pomegranate Hour's 2018
grant plaque, VEIL.tv's lapse "in vol 5," and the 2002
confession-booth reference as recent-past. No arcana-layer
knots found — this stratum reconciles as-is.

## PART II · THE OLAF STRAND **[REC — Knot 1, the big one]**

The generational strand (Olaf/Eddvard/Marit/Olaus/Per/Roy) is
woven from incompatible drafts in the prose itself. This draft
proposes the **shifted resolution**: keep the living characters'
stated ages (Per 60, Marit 68, Roy mid-sixties, Aud 73, Hans
68), and move the strand's historical dates forward ~32 years.
Under it:

- **~1996** — Olaf Egilsson (b. ~1970) and Eddvard (b. ~1959)
  meet at the old-Yaquina cutter base *(prose: 1964)*.
- **~2003–2006** — Olaf carves Marit's bowl, a little every
  Sunday *(prose: 1971–74)*; gives it to her on her 34th
  birthday, **2006** (Marit b. 1972 → 68 in 2052 ✓).
- **~2005** — Olaf's Bern deployment; meets Werner Junior;
  the three notebooks *(prose: 1973)*.
- **~2008** — the network bowls: Eddvard's, Henrik Lindholm's
  (San Francisco; Henrik d. 2018 — a ten-year window, tight but
  workable), the bell-piece, Olaus's *(prose: 1976–77)*.
- **November 2010** — **Olaf and Olaus drown off Yaquina Head**
  *(prose: November '78)*. Per is 18 (b. 1992 → 60 in 2052 ✓);
  Olaf is 40 ✓.
- **2011** — the cabin built by Eddvard with Per, 19, helping
  weekends from Oregon State *(prose: 1979)*; Eddvard is 52 ✓
  and dies 2042 at 83 ✓. The copper line follows (~2013).
- **~2018** — Per, having broken into Olaf's office at 19
  (~2011), burns the notebook *(prose: 1986)*.
- **~2021** — Werner Junior hand-prints the poem cycle *Bowls*;
  Hans (b. 1984 → 68 in 2052 ✓) is 37 when it arrives ✓
  *(prose: November 1989)*.
- Aria's face has been inside Marit's bowl "since 2006" *(prose:
  1974)* — which places the first carving of her face **the
  same decade Dean is building the tower for her**. The strand
  and the Dean strand converge instead of running a century
  apart. This is the argument for the shifted resolution.

**What the shifted resolution costs:** the Estuary gallery's
submission dates for O. Egilsson (1971→~2003), E. Halvorsen
(1968→~2000), and M. Egilsson (1974→~2006) move; Werner
Senior's 1960s printing "to a Coast Guardsman named Olaf"
becomes Werner Junior's from the late '90s; the gallery's
W. Schenck Sr. 1953 and H. Lindholm 1969 builds stay (they are
the older generations and still work). Change-inventory: ~8
scene files (ch10_cabin, ch10_per, ch11_per, ch11_bowl,
ch13_porch, ch15_bread, ch17_roy, epilogue_gallery) — **VN
prose edits, not to be made until the author approves.**

**The alternative (elder resolution):** keep every historical
date as printed and correct the ages instead — Per ~92, Marit
~112, Roy's dates re-read as 19xx. Preserves the gallery's
century-spread; requires deck-canon longevity and re-aging
three on-page characters. Costs fewer files but changes who
the characters physically are. **This draft recommends the
shifted resolution**; the author decides.
