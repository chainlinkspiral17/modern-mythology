HOW TO APPROVE A SONG
  1. ⌂ LINK GAME FOLDER (top left) → pick the godot folder inside your checkout (…/mm-fm1/godot) → Chrome asks to let the page edit files → Allow. The badge then reads GAME FOLDER: godot. After a restart the button may say ⌂ ALLOW GAME FOLDER: tap it once. Without this, APPROVE can't put anything in the game.
  2. Type your name in who (top right) — it goes into the approval record.
  3. Pick a track in the list on the left. CANDIDATES shows tracks with takes waiting for a verdict; MISSING shows tracks with no music yet.
  4. No takes yet? In the box under TAKES set how many (1–6), optionally write a DIRECTION (“slower, a lonely piano over rain”), then ⚄ MAKE TAKES. A DAW tab opens and renders them — leave it open; the takes appear here when done (⟳ RESCAN if they don't).
  5. Listen: ▶ PLAY on a take (or keys 1–6). The intensity buttons show how its layers come in as a scene gets tense; ▶ PLAY AS SCENE plays it against a real scene.
  6. Like one? Optionally type why in the notes field, then ✓ APPROVE on that take (key a). If another take was approved before, tap APPROVE a second time within 4 s to replace it. The take shows APPROVED and the game plays it from now on.
  7. Don't want one? ✕ REJECT, then tap it again to confirm (key x twice). Tick reject deletes files to delete its audio too. Rejected seeds are never made again.
  8. None good? Change the DIRECTION / STYLE / ENERGY / INSTRUMENTS and ⚄ MAKE TAKES again. ⟳ AGAIN remakes one take from its saved settings.
  9. Changed your mind later: ✕ REVOKE APPROVED TEMP (tap twice) takes it back out of the game. Real recorded audio is never touched.
  10. When you're done: back the music up (it never goes in git) and commit the approval list — see the commands at the bottom of the page / from Claude.
