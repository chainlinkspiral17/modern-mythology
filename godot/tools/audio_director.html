HOW TO APPROVE A SONG
  1. ⌂ LINK GAME FOLDER (top left) → pick the godot folder inside your checkout (…/mm-fm1/godot) → Chrome asks to let the page edit files → Allow. The badge then reads GAME FOLDER: godot. After a restart the button may say ⌂ ALLOW GAME FOLDER: tap it once. Without this, APPROVE can't put anything in the game.
  2. Type your name in who (top right) — it goes into the approval record.
  3. Pick a track in the list on the left. CANDIDATES shows tracks with takes waiting for a verdict; MISSING shows tracks with no music yet.
  4. No takes yet? Tap ⚄ MAKE TAKES in the TAKES header. To steer them first, ⚙ SETTINGS ↓ jumps to the box where you set how many (1–6) and can write a DIRECTION (“slower, a lonely piano over rain”). They are composed and rendered right here — the message under the button counts through each take (composing → rendering stems → saving), and each one appears under TAKES as soon as it's done. Keep this page open while it works.
  5. Steering the takes (all optional — blank / auto means the brief decides): a QUICK chip (lonely night, chase, dread, neon …) fills the panel with a flavour and the line under it says what it set; 🎲 SURPRISE ME rolls a style, an ensemble that suits it, a feel, a harmony and a sound. ENSEMBLE picks an instrument family (orchestra, jazz, rock, choir, chiptune, lo-fi …) and each take draws its own line-up from it. VARIETY: close keeps style and instruments and changes only the notes, mixed varies the instruments, wide gives each take after the first a different but related style, wild anything. MUSIC sets KEY (or the volume's home key), BPM, FEEL (straight, swung, shuffle, half / double time), HARMONY (plain, sevenths, lush 9ths, open, modal) and the PROGression family. MORE holds the sound design (ambience beds, risers, hits), parts, extra pads and the seed; INSTRUMENTS picks one role by hand and wins over the ensemble.
  6. Listen: ▶ PLAY on a take (or keys 1–6). The intensity buttons show how its layers come in as a scene gets tense; ▶ PLAY AS SCENE plays it against a real scene.
  7. Like one? Optionally type why in the notes field, then ✓ APPROVE on that take (key a). If another take was approved before, tap APPROVE a second time within 4 s to replace it. If the track already has a .wav that isn't a temp (a recording, or an older DAW bounce), the first tap says what it is and a second tap moves it to assets/audio/replaced/ (kept, not deleted) before approving. APPROVE turns the take into small Ogg Vorbis files for the game (a few seconds — the message counts up; about a tenth of the WAV size). The take then shows APPROVED and the game plays it from now on.
  8. Don't want one? ✕ REJECT, then tap it again to confirm (key x twice). Tick reject deletes files to delete its audio too. Rejected seeds are never made again.
  9. REMIX (under MAKE TAKES) makes takes for this track out of music that's already done: pick a take (any track's — an approved one is marked), a recording or an approved temp under FROM, a RECIPE, then ⤳ REMIX. RESTYLE / COVER / VARIATIONS / UNDERSCORE / INTENSE / DREAM / BLEND re-compose the take's own chords, sections and theme (they need a take made here); CHOP / DUB / STRETCH / TAPE cut up the audio itself (anything that plays) over new drums and bass. Every take row has ⤳ REMIX to start from it. Remixes land under TAKES like any other take.
  10. None good? Change the DIRECTION / STYLE / ENSEMBLE / VARIETY / ENERGY / INSTRUMENTS and ⚄ MAKE TAKES again. ⟳ AGAIN remakes one take from its saved settings (its style, line-up, feel and sound — the card shows what it got). Want to change notes, instruments or the mix by hand? ✎ EDIT IN DAW opens that take in the DAW (tap START there); when it sounds right, ⇪ SEND TO DIRECTOR (top of the DAW) renders it and it shows up here as a new take, ready to play and approve.
  11. Changed your mind later: ✕ REVOKE APPROVED TEMP (tap twice) takes it back out of the game. Real recorded audio is never touched.
  12. Songs approved before the Ogg change are still big WAVs in the game: ⇣ SHRINK … WAV TEMPS → OGG (top bar) re-encodes all of them in one go and deletes the WAVs. Only temps — never real audio.
  13. When you're done: back the music up (it never goes in git) and commit the approval list — see the commands at the bottom of the page / from Claude.
