TAKES
REPLACE: a take replaces what was in its region · OVERDUB: it adds to it (audio: a layer on top) · OVERDUB LOOP: every loop pass adds into one MIDI take. With ⟲ LOOP on, each pass is its own take (pick them in the takes row under the track); PUNCH limits recording to the red region on the ruler.
LATENCY
FM-1 RETURNms
Audio of the FM-1 coming back on its own USB audio — played by the DAW over MIDI, or on its keys. MIDI out → synth → USB audio in, measured by FM-1 CHECK-OUT (step 7).
PLAY-ALONG INPUTms
Any other input (mic, guitar, line) recording you playing along to the playback: output + input latency. LOOPBACK: cable the output into the input picked in EXPORT → RECORDING (or hold the mic to the speaker), then press it.
MIDI NOTESms
FM-1 keys, any MIDI keyboard, the computer keys: notes land where they were heard, by each event's own time stamp. This is only an extra nudge (normally 0).
