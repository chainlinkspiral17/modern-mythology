# ESTUARY 2
### Oneironautics Inc. · 2002 · simulation
### the summer at the mudflats · witnessing as mechanic

---

## THE BOX COPY

*Twelve walks on one tidal flat, June to September. You have a
field journal, four neighbors, a crank radio on the boardwalk
rail, and no lever at all this time. Nothing on the flat is
yours to manage.*

*In week six, the county drives in four orange stakes. What
happens after that will depend, more than anyone expects, on
how carefully you have been looking.*

---

## GETTING STARTED

Estuary 2 sits on the slowstick shelf under Volume 7, in the
tall Betamax-style case. Boot it and choose JUNE · THE FIRST
WALK, or CONTINUE — the game saves after every walk, and the
title page keeps count of the summers you have witnessed.

**Controls:** arrows or WASD to walk. **E** to interact — hold
it near a creature to LOOK, tap it at the radio to CRANK, at a
neighbor to TALK, at the county road to go home and end the
walk. **J** opens the field journal. ESC puts the summer down.

## HOW TO PLAY · THE WALK

Twelve walks, one per week. Each walk you roam the flat as long
as you like — the channel, the boardwalk, the pickleweed, the
four house-backs on the bluff — and go home when you choose, at
the county road. The weather changes weekly (the crank radio
speaks it, in vowels, doing its best) and the weather drives the
populations. You affect nothing directly. That is the design,
not an oversight.

## LOOKING IS THE VERB

- **Hold E near a species** until the ring closes: one
  observation, logged. Twelve species live on this flat.
- **Attention is finite.** You can spend **four** observations
  per walk, and the flat holds more than four things worth
  seeing. Which ones you follow is the game. Attention refreshes
  each walk; the scarcity is in the moment.
- **Three walks running** on the same species and its journal
  page ILLUMINATES — fully drawn, its population math visible on
  the page, its voice added to that walk's music. Twelve pages
  is the collectible. Nobody needs all twelve.
- The flat remembers absences. Lose a species for a few walks
  and find it again, and the journal will say so.

## THE STAKES · from walk six

Orange — the most saturated color in the game. The county wants
the channel dredged for the marina upcoast. From walk six a
petition can be walked door to door:

- **RUTH** (crabber) reads both sides and hands it back. Her
  boat drafts too deep. Respect her for it.
- **COLE** (retired county engineer) will sign, but not until
  something he has been embarrassed to say gets said. Keep
  talking to him, late in the summer.
- **MRS. AMES** (owns the boardwalk) is counting your journal
  pages. Show her a summer of looking — six illuminated pages —
  and she signs above the line, neatly.
- **JULES** (early twenties, watches the flat like television)
  signs before you finish the sentence.

## THE HEARING · after walk ten

A folding-chair room and a county seal. You get one statement,
and the statement is assembled from your illuminated journal
pages — the game quietly requires that you WITNESSED to have
standing. Three illuminated pages gets you the floor at all.
The county then weighs your pages (six at most will fit in one
statement) together with the signatures you carried in. Then it
votes, and you walk the last two weeks knowing the result.

## WINTER *(spoilers · read after the vote)*

The summer ends on one of three winter cards:

- **DREDGED** — a straight channel; the journal pages fade at
  the edges. This is where weak cases and absent witnesses land.
- **THE COMPROMISE** — the half-dredge. The card refuses
  judgment. Five points of pages-plus-signatures buys it.
- **BLOCKED** — the flat as you knew it, and Ruth no longer
  waves. It takes nearly everything: eight points of pages and
  signatures together, which means a deep journal AND most of
  the doors.

The title page tallies every summer's verdict. The flat keeps
working either way, without you, which is the point.

## THE FINE PRINT

*Oneironautics Inc. removed the lever on purpose and has read
all the letters about it. This cartridge contains twelve species,
four neighbors, one hearing, and no way to win that does not
pass through paying attention. The radio's weather service is
performed by a radio doing its best. Estuary 2 is dedicated to
everyone who ever filled out a public comment card and stayed
for the whole meeting.*
