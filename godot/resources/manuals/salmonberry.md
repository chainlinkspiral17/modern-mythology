# SALMONBERRY
### Oneironautics Inc. · 1992 · part RPG · part adventure
### a year in a town the berry is named for

---

## THE BOX COPY

*September, 1963. Your mother put you on the bus with one suitcase
and her mother's address. Salmonberry, Oregon — four streets, one
cannery, a bay with a bar at its mouth, and a grandmother you have
met twice. You have ten months until June.*

*Nobody in this town needs you. That is the first thing you will
fix.*

---

## GETTING STARTED

Salmonberry lives on the slowstick shelf under Volume 7. Boot the
cartridge and choose NEW YEAR, or pick up a saved autumn where you
left it — the game saves after every week.

**Controls:** everything is a choice on screen. Arrow keys or
d-pad to move focus, accept to choose, ESC (or B) to put the year
down and keep your place. WALK INTO TOWN opens the walkable town —
WASD or left stick to walk, E or A to talk to whoever is at the
place you stop.

## HOW TO PLAY · THE WEEK

A month is four weeks. A week is one decision — but every decision
leans on five things at once:

- **ENERGY (the dots, 0–10).** Every activity costs it; hard work
  costs more than a week of sleep gives back (+2). Go in worn thin
  and the week knows it: −2 on the work, and the text will say so.
  *Keep to the house* restores 5 and gives Vovo an evening — rest
  is a real choice, not a skipped turn.
- **WEATHER.** Rolled per week from the month's climate, fixed to
  your save (reloading does not reroll the sky). Storms close the
  bay outright — no rowing, no clamming, with the reason shown.
  Minus tides throw the flats open. Rain taxes outdoor work unless
  you bought the slicker. **The glass forecasts one week ahead.**
  Plan on it.
- **MONEY.** Board is owed to the household: $2 into the flour tin
  every month. An empty tin strains the house and costs you with
  Vovo — and three strained months follow you into the year's
  last page.
- **THE CHECK.** Every activity rolls your aptitude + luck (0–2,
  seeded) + weather and gear against the work's difficulty, and
  the roll is SHOWN: *"sea 2 + luck 1 +2 minus tide vs. the work
  2 — a strong week."* Strong weeks pay bonuses. Rough weeks pay
  half and teach grit. Both are the game.
- **BONDS.** Eight people worth knowing. Bonds open new activities
  at 2/4/6, and a friendship left unattended for two months slips
  a point — with a line, so you feel it. Maintenance is play.

## THE GENERAL STORE

Browsing does not spend the week. Five things on the wall, each of
which changes what your weeks can do:

| gear | $ | what it changes |
|---|---|---|
| clam gun | 4 | +2 on the flats, +$1 |
| rain slicker | 5 | rain stops taxing outdoor work |
| bicycle | 8 | +1 energy back every week |
| guitar strings | 3 | songs with Vovo count double |
| field glasses | 6 | +1 outdoors |

## THE CALENDAR · this week only

Nine dated things happen exactly once and never again: the fall
run's biggest day, the grange dance, the vigil, Christmas Eve at
the gray house, the lowest tide of the year, a boat in trouble off
the bar, rebuilding week, Salmonberry Sunday, the travelling show.
They are marked with a star and a warning. Take them or lose them.

**The boat off the bar is the year's one real gamble** — a shown
check with a genuine failure branch. Going matters either way.
What happens if you succeed follows you all the way to March.

## THE QUIET THREAD

Someone in this town keeps a light in a window, and the town does
not say why. Four clues exist — behind deep bonds and certain
quiet errands. Follow the thread or don't; the book of the coast
keeps what you learn. It pays off on the one night the whole year
bends around.

## MARCH

We will say only this: build the year like it matters, because on
Good Friday, 1964, it will.

## THE REGISTERS *(spoilers · read after June)*

The year ends by naming who the town knows in June. Five registers:

- **SONG** — Vovo's bond ≥6 and three songs learned.
- **KEEPER** — nine of the book's twelve pages filled.
- **LISTENER** — heart+word ≥10 and three deep friendships.
- **HANDS** — hands+sea+grit ≥12 and two town-deep bonds.
- **LEAVER** — none of the above. The bus runs both ways.

The coda reads the wave night, the boat off the bar, the thread,
and the flour tin back at you.

## THE FINE PRINT

*Oneironautics Inc. thanks you for a year of your time, which is
the only currency this cartridge accepts. Salmonberry is a real
kind of place but not a real place. The tide tables are accurate
for a coast that does not exist. No dogs, grandmothers, or
salmonberries were harmed. The bar at the mouth of the bay does
not care how brave you are — it cares whether the line reaches.*
