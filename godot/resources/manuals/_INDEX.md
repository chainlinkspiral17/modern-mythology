# MODERN MYTHOLOGY · MANUALS & GUIDES
### the whole package, documented

This folder holds a manual or guide for every game in Modern
Mythology: the visual novel's reader guide, the two big insets,
and one manual per slowstick on the Volume 7 shelf. The slowstick
manuals are written the way their in-fiction studios would have
written them — box copy, how-to-play, and fine print in each
studio's own voice — wrapped around accurate guides to the systems
as they exist in the current build.

## The frame

- **[Reading Modern Mythology](reading_modern_mythology.md)** —
  the visual novel, vols 5–7: presentation grammar, choices,
  settings, screenshots, and how everything else is reached.
- **[Tarot Gauntlet](tarot_gauntlet.md)** — the vol 5 inset. One
  arcana at a time, a real place each, the bundle and the leap.
- **[Community Planned](community_planned.md)** — the vol 6 inset.
  Four regions, the agents, the demons, the BBS, the tower.

## House rules that hold across every manual

- **Spoilers live only under headings marked *(spoilers)*.** Every
  manual is safe to read before playing down to that line.
- **The systems are current.** Manuals describe the builds as they
  are now — including the 2026-07 depth passes (Salmonberry's week
  loop, Northwind's kindness chains, Estuary 4's working season,
  the Tideline's tide clock, Mrs. Wu's weather and linen chest,
  Sam's register shifts, Spiderdrops' living rain).
- **Cartridges talk to each other.** Some sticks read what you did
  in others, and one does not appear on the shelf until you have
  learned why it exists. The manuals say this much and no more.
- **F4** hides every overlay in every game, for clean pictures.
