# SPIDERDROPS 2 · THE LONG WIND
### Pretty Dreams · Beaverton, OR · 1995 · arcade · physics
### the storm is over · seven legs of sky · "let go"

---

## THE BOX COPY

*NOW YOU FLY! The storm has passed and the spider is done
holding on. Cast your silk into the wind and BALLOON — over the
gaps, past the debris, leg after leg, toward somewhere new!*

*The wind that took the web is the wind that carries you.
That's not in the manual because it's sad. It's in the manual
because it's how ballooning works. Ask any spider.*

---

## GETTING STARTED

The Long Wind does not appear on the shelf until you have
weathered Spiderdrops — you cannot own the sequel before you
finish the original. Boot the cartridge and CATCH THE WIND. ESC
sets down mid-journey; the save keeps your best register and
your journey count.

**What you carry in:** the title screen reads how you left the
first storm, and your starting silk is that ending, stated in
mechanics — leave THE WEB HELD and you set out with a full
pouch; leave HELD and it's most of one, a little worn; leave THE
STORM and you set out with almost nothing but yourself. The
hardest, most honest start. (Never played the storm? You get a
traveler's ration.) And if you left the storm with a certain
eight-pointed shape, it rides with you, a small gold glint on
the abdomen.

**Controls:** HOLD accept/A to lift on the silk · ↑ / ↓ to steer
· that's the whole machine. The rest is wind.

## HOW TO PLAY · SEVEN LEGS

The journey is **seven legs**. Each leg has two halves:

**SETTLE.** You are on a branch. Rest — silk refills fast while
you sit. Cast whenever you're ready (there's a breath's minimum;
even spiders check the wind first), but every second of rest is
silk in the pouch. When the banner says CAST, press accept and
the wind takes you.

**FLY.** You hang from a ballooning streamer and the wind pushes
you along the leg.

- **THE WIND BAND** — the bright stripe across the middle of the
  sky. Inside it, the wind carries you at full speed. Stall
  above it, fade below it. Sit in the stripe.
- **LIFTING COSTS SILK.** Holding accept fights gravity and pays
  for it out of the pouch. Steering up costs a little; steering
  down is free, the way down always is.
- **GUSTS.** The wind still comes in waves. When the banner says
  *GUST — ride it*, the band is at its strongest — the same
  gusts that tore the old web apart, working for you now.
- **THE CHURN** — the dark rain-boil at the bottom of the sky.
  Drift down into it and it **drinks your silk** and drags you
  heavier. Get out.
- **DEBRIS** still flies. A hit knocks you downward and costs a
  bite of silk.

## WHAT THE SKY GIVES BACK

The sky takes silk. The sky also gives it, if you fly where the
giving is:

- **AIRBORNE DROPS** hang low, just above the churn. Dip out of
  the fast band and drink them — **+8 silk each** — at the cost
  of speed, and at the risk of the churn right below. The best
  silk is always kept next to the worst air.
- **GOLD THERMALS** — twice a leg, a shimmer of rising gold
  scrolls past, once, and does not come again. Inside it,
  **lifting is free** and the column pushes you upward on top of
  it. An expiring window. Take it or watch it go.

Reach the far branch and the leg is crossed; settle, breathe,
cast again. Seven times.

## HOW IT ENDS *(spoilers)*

There are no game-overs in the sky. There are two ways down:

- **STILL FLYING** — the silk runs dry mid-air, anywhere along
  the seven legs. The last of it pays out and you stop steering.
  This is not a loss. The wind has you now, and it is going
  somewhere you were not, and that turns out to be all right.
  You are not lost. You are only still flying.
- **A NEW TREE** — cross all seven legs. The wind sets you down
  on a branch you have never seen, in a place the storm never
  reached, with silk enough to begin again. You can always begin
  again. This is the note the first game refused to play.
- **UNDER THE EAVES** — cross all seven legs *carrying the
  eight-pointed star in from the first game*, and the last gust
  carries you somewhere else instead: in under a low roof, out
  of the weather at last, the corner of a cabin by the water.
  Something is already there, waiting where the wall meets the
  wall. You are home, in a house that is not yours, and no one
  will ever tell you whose.

## THE FINE PRINT

*Pretty Dreams presents the only sequel in its catalog that is
easier to win and harder to talk about. Ballooning is real;
spiders really do this; nobody tells them where they'll land
either. The gold shimmer is not sponsored. The churn is not
angry, it is just the churn. If you finish with silk to spare,
that was resting, not luck. This one you can win. We did not
believe it the first time it happened either.*
